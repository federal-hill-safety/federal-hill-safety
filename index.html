Data & methodology

ItemDetail
Crime data sourceBaltimore PD NIBRS Group A Crime Data via Open Baltimore, Jan 2022–Jul 31, 2026.
911 data sourceBPD Calls for Service, 2023–2026 via Open Baltimore, through Jul 31, 2026. Pre-filtered to Federal Hill, Fells Point, Downtown, Canton, and adjacent neighborhoods.
Violent crime categoriesCommon assault, aggravated assault, homicide, robbery, robbery-carjacking, rape, sex offenses. Robbery-commercial excluded.
911 calls: resident vs. patrolThe Bar-Hours 911 tab counts resident/citizen-initiated calls only, excluding: Foot Patrol, Business Check, Bike Patrol, Directed Patrol (2023's legacy "DIRECTED PATROL" category folded in), Traffic Stop, Private Tow, Silent Alarm, Audible Alarm, RA Police. Everything else — disorderly, common assault, noise complaint, disturbance, narcotics, DWI, check well-being, hit and run, aggravated assault, etc. — counts as resident-initiated demand.
Time zone adjustmentNIBRS timestamps are UTC and are converted to Eastern via tz_localize(UTC).tz_convert(America/New_York), which correctly handles the EST/EDT switch. 911 timestamps are already Eastern in all years; the 2024 file's "+00" suffix is a platform display artifact, not a real UTC offset, and is stripped rather than converted.
Bar hoursThu 8pm–4am, Fri 8pm–4am, Sat 8pm–4am, Sun 8pm–4am ET (each window running into the following morning). 32 hrs/week.
Robbery deduplicationWhen ROBBERY and ROBBERY-COMMERCIAL share a case number, only ROBBERY is retained. Robbery cases with multiple named victims are not deduplicated further — each victim record counts as its own incident, the same convention used for assault, rape, and sex offenses.
District comparison projection factorsEach district's factor = average of (full-year ÷ Jan 1–Jul 31 count) across 2023–2025, computed from that district's own bar-hours violent corridor data. Federal Hill ×1.7297 · Fells Point ×1.7503 · Downtown/The Block ×1.6346 · Canton ×1.3824. Applied to each district's 2026 YTD (Jan 1–Jul 31) count to produce the 2026 full-year projection shown in the District Comparison bar chart and scorecards.
